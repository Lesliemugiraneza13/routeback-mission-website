## Alternative transport providers

Contact details (website, phone, email, booking/contact/sign-in links) for the ten providers listed on the Services page were sourced directly from each provider's own public site, cross-referenced against `images/originals/Mauritius_Private_Transport_Providers.pdf`. Logos for MoRide, Taxi Service Mauritius, Taxi Mauritius Transfer, Yugo Mauritius, DodoGo, Island Taxi Mauritius and Uber Mauritius are the providers' own supplied logo files. Barefoot Transfers Mauritius, My PRORIDE and Ala‑lila had no logo file supplied, so a plain neutral wordmark tile stands in for each, built in the site's own type system rather than an invented brand mark.

## What is deliberately *not* claimed

- No live bus tracking, live traffic, or live arrival times.
- No guaranteed transfer times between connecting routes.
- No verified walking time from the Decathlon Bus Stop to the ALCHE campus.
- No ranking of alternative transport providers by price, speed, or safety.
